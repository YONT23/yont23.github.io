Past Participle of Irregular Verbs ♡ Grade 8

Remember: Present Perfect = have / has + past participle | Past Participle = the third form of irregular verbs.

1 I ____________ to Paris three times.

2 She ____________ her homework before dinner.

3 We ____________ that movie last night.

4 They ____________ the new restaurant in town.

5 I haven't ____________ my keys. I can't find them.

6 My grandfather ____________ that house for 20 years.

7 She ____________ a lot of progress in her English class.

8 He ____________ the invitation yet.

9 We ____________ the results of the exam.

10 I ____________ the book yet, but I want to read it.
