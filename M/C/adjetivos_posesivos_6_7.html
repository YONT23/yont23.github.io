Possessive Adjectives

Adjetivos Posesivos en Inglés

Grados 6° - 7°

Recuerda / Remember

Los adjetivos posesivos indican a quién pertenece algo y siempre van antes de un sustantivo.

PronombreAdjetivo posesivoEjemplo
Imymy book
youyouryour pencil
hehishis dog
sheherher cat
ititsits tail
weourour school
theytheirtheir house

Ejercicio 1: Completa la oración

Escribe el adjetivo posesivo correcto.

1. I have a red bag. ______ bag is new.

2. Maria has a cat. ______ cat is black.

3. Carlos has a bike. ______ bike is fast.

4. We have a classroom. ______ classroom is big.

5. The students have books. ______ books are on the table.

6. The dog is hungry. ______ food is in the kitchen.

7. Ana, is this ______ notebook?

Ejercicio 2: Selección múltiple

Haz clic en la opción correcta.

1. Pedro loves ______ family.

2. This is ______ school. We study here.

3. The bird has wings. ______ wings are blue.

4. Look at those children. ______ toys are on the floor.

5. I live with my parents. ______ house is near the school.
