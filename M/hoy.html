Future Perfect & Future Continuous in Professional Contexts

🎯 B2 / C1 ⏱ 2h académicas 📅 Octubre 2026
👨‍🏫

Teacher Explanation

▼

The Future Selfie: El docente pide a los alumnos imaginar dónde estarán exactamente a esta hora el próximo año, y qué estarán haciendo. La transición al mundo post-escolar requiere de un plan de vida estructurado.

Grammar Tetris: Es vital diferenciar las acciones futuras en progreso de las metas futuras ya completadas[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Future Perfect es una "foto" de la meta alcanzada (check ✅), mientras que Future Continuous es un "video" de la acción sucediendo en ese momento[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

Al hablar de nuestro plan de carrera, usaremos bloques inalterables: [WILL HAVE + V3] para el Perfect, y [WILL BE + V-ING] para el Continuous. Si mezclas las piezas (ej: will been working ❌), pierdes profesionalismo.

🎭

Classroom Simulation — "The 2030 Time Capsule"

▼

En grupos, los alumnos diseñan cómo será su vida profesional para el 2030. Deben compartir 3 hitos que habrán terminado (Perfect) y 3 procesos en marcha (Continuous)[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

👨‍🏫
Docente
Let's open the 2030 Time Capsule. By the time we meet again in four years, what will have changed? Give me milestones and ongoing processes!
Mateo
By 2030, I will have graduated with my degree in software engineering. That's a major milestone.
👨‍🎓
👨‍🏫
Docente
Great photo of the finish line, Mateo. And what will the "video" look like? What will you be doing daily?
Mateo
I will be working for an international tech company, and I'll probably be managing my own startup on the side.
👨‍🎓

Grammar Focus

▼
Future Perfect: Subject + WILL HAVE + Past Participle (V3)

By the end of this year, I will have finished my high school studies. (Completed action)

Future Continuous: Subject + WILL BE + V-ing

This time next September, I will be attending university lectures. (Action in progress)

🧠

Visual Thinking

▼
       YOUR PROFESSIONAL ROADMAP

  📸 FUTURE PERFECT = The Photo (Completed Milestone)
     [WILL + HAVE + V3]
     "By 2030, I will have earned my degree." ✅ (Check!)

  🎥 FUTURE CONTINUOUS = The Video (Daily Reality)
     [WILL + BE + V-ING]
     "In 2030, I will be living in Bogota." 🔄 (In motion)

  ⛔ FATAL ERRORS TO AVOID IN INTERVIEWS:
  ❌ "I will been working" (Don't mix the blocks)
  ❌ "I will have finish" (Don't forget the V3/ED ending)

Reading Practice

▼
A Letter to My High School Self If I could speak to myself back in 11th grade, I would tell him to stop worrying so much about the exact timeline of success. Back then, I thought that by age 25, I would have already figured everything out. I assumed I would have bought an apartment and that I would be directing a huge marketing agency. The reality is a bit different, but equally exciting. Right now, I am 25. I haven't bought a house yet, but by the end of this year, I will have paid off my student loans, which is a massive milestone. Next month, I will be moving to Montreal to start a master's degree. While my friends here will be working their usual 9-to-5 jobs, I will be navigating a new culture and learning French. The career path is rarely a straight line. By the time you reach your goals, your goals will have evolved. Enjoy the journey.
👂

Listening Practice

▼
Cierra los ojos y concéntrate en identificar los tiempos verbales.
"When they ask me where I see myself in ten years, I like to be specific. Ten years from now, I will be thirty-one years old. I won't be working in a traditional office. Instead, I will be traveling globally as an environmental consultant. By that time, I will have published my research on sustainable urban planning, and I will have built a strong network of professionals who share my vision. It requires a lot of hard work today, but I know that this time next decade, I will be living exactly the life I am designing right now."

Homework — "My Personal Roadmap"

▼

📝 Assignment

  • El Reto: Escribe un ensayo breve (10-12 oraciones) detallando tu visión profesional y personal a 5 años (para el año 2031).
  • Requisitos de gramática: Debes incluir estrictamente 3 oraciones en Future Perfect detallando logros consolidados (ej. I will have graduated...) y 3 oraciones en Future Continuous describiendo tu estilo de vida o trabajo diario (ej. I will be designing...).
  • Vocabulario: Usa al menos tres palabras de la lista de la clase (milestone, journey, timeline, achievement).
