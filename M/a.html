English Workshop: Unit 3 - People and Possessions

Institución Educativa Siglo XXI | Grado 6° - Nivel A1

Punto 1: Family & Possessives Lingüística - Gramática

Selecciona la opción correcta (HIS, HER o 'S) para completar cada oración sobre la familia Simpsons y Madrigal:

1. Homer is the father. car is red.
2. Luisa is Mirabel's sister. arms are big!
3. My name is Roberto. (El nombre de mi papá)
4. Bruno is Mirabel's uncle. room is big and dark.

Punto 2: Spot the Room Lingüística - Vocabulario y Ubicación

Completa las oraciones usando There is, There are, in, on o under:

1. one bed in the bedroom.
2. two pillows on the bed.
3. The cat is sleeping the bed (debajo de la cama).
4. My pencils are my backpack (dentro del bolso).

Punto 3: Listening Challenge Comunicativa - Listening

Escucha el audio haciendo clic en el botón y selecciona la respuesta correcta:

1. What does Chef Pedro hate?
2. Does Chef María like chocolate?

Punto 4: Food Preferences Reading Comunicativa - Reading

Lee el siguiente texto adaptado y responde a las preguntas de comprensión:

"My name is Laura. I love food! My favorite food is pizza. I also like chicken and rice. My brother Carlos is different. He hates vegetables! He doesn't like broccoli or tomatoes. But he loves hamburgers."
1. What is Laura's favorite food?
2. What vegetables does Carlos hate? (Menciona al menos uno)

Punto 5: MasterChef Short Report Comunicativa - Writing

Escribe un texto corto (3 a 5 oraciones) describiendo los gustos de comida de un familiar. Usa likes, loves, o doesn't like:

Ejemplo: "My mother's name is Carmen. She loves chicken. She likes rice, but she doesn't like broccoli."
